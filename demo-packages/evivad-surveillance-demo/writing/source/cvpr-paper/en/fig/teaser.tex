\captionof{figure}{\textbf{Overview of \evivad{} (illustrative only)}. Existing training-free large-model pipelines (a) emit a single anomaly score, hiding misses, false alarms and hallucinated explanations. \evivad{} (b) builds three complementary modules on top of a \emph{fully frozen} vision-language backbone---DAA (low-rank domain adaptation), EAD (evidence-anchored decoding) and DAG (degradation-aware gating)---and returns four outputs: a frame-level score curve, temporal intervals, an evidence-cited explanation, and confidence/abstention flags. (c) The aggregate results indicate that detection accuracy and explanation verifiability improve simultaneously.}
